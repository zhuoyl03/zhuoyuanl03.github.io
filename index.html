Testing the Transferability of Critical-Satellite Rankings in LEO Networks

Zhuoyuan Li

Manuscript · 2026

Can a failure ranking be reused after the routing policy changes? I built a controlled evaluation that removes satellites individually and measures how much target-policy damage a reused ranking captures, rather than relying on ranking overlap alone.

Tests across 24 orbital phases identify a specific transfer loss from utilization-aware routing to IGP-ECMP for peak-link-stress damage. Shortest-distance and hop-count rankings, in contrast, transfer well for propagation damage.

LEO Satellite Network Resilience Analysis: A Focus on Critical Satellites

Zhuoyuan Li*, Wenyi (Morty) Zhang*, Wenhao Chen, Yiyan Hu, Weyl Lu

ACM LEO-NET 2024 · * Equal contribution

Some heavily used satellites lie over high-latitude oceans, far from dense ground-station regions. Our Starlink- and Kuiper-shell simulations show how their removal redistributes traffic and increases end-to-end delay.

As a co-first author, I extended the Hypatia-based simulation and analysis pipeline for satellite selection, targeted failures, path recomputation, and RTT analysis.

Risk-Aware Decision Routing under Limited Review Capacity

Zhuoyuan Li

Research project · 2026

I built a system that allocates a fixed review budget across disaster images, comparing uncertainty-based routing with learned estimates of average and tail error costs.

At 20% simulated review capacity, tail-risk routing left 34% fewer severe errors but incurred 35% higher weighted error cost than entropy routing. A separate adoption check did not support a switch, so the system retains the entropy baseline.

CLIP adaptation for disaster-response classification

Purdue VIP AI4SMA · Spring 2026

With Yubeen Oh, Akhilesh Tuttagunta, and Logan Fossum

I investigated inconsistent image-text labels in CrisisMMD, implemented agreement-based filtering, and consolidated eight humanitarian categories into five. On this filtered task, head-only CLIP training reached 83.9% macro-F1, compared with 70.9% for LoRA and 79.7% for full fine-tuning.
